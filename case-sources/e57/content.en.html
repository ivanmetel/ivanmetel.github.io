01 / The task

Clients judge the service before work begins

A client enquires about fit-out work. A detailed design may not yet exist, but the company still needs to assess the scope of work and propose terms.

Quality workmanship matters, but clients assess the company before work starts: by its response time, attention to detail and the accuracy of its proposal. To deliver on those expectations, the workflow must connect property data, cost calculations and presentation.

02 / Property data

From point cloud
to floor plan

Cost calculations depend on accurate measurements. The company needs a reasonable contingency for possible additional work; the client needs a predictable price without an excessive buffer.

Interpreting the point cloud turns scan data into property geometry: we identify walls and openings, define room boundaries, and produce a floor plan and room schedule. Measurement accuracy affects both the quantities of work and the cost calculations.

A professional scan of an apartment of around 100 m² in Cyprus costs about €1,500. A compact LiDAR scanner costs roughly the same as one such service. It is accurate enough for estimating smaller projects, so the company can avoid commissioning a professional scan for each property and passing that cost on to the client.
