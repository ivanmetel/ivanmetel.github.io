A measured basis

We measure the building to quantify the work.

Every site visit combines LiDAR capture and expert inspection. We build a measured model from the scan, use it to study accessible areas and calculate quantities, and use the inspection findings to explain which works are needed for your plans.

Scope of assessment

What we can verify on site.

LiDAR records accessible geometry. Expert inspection assesses visible conditions. The report marks areas with limited access and findings that need separate investigation.

  • Hidden constructionConcealed defects are not treated as inspected without opening-up or a separate survey.
  • System operationEquipment function needs the appropriate safe test and qualified specialist.
  • Structural capacityVisible warning signs can be recorded; structural calculations require a structural engineer.
  • Investment returnsNo rent, occupancy, resale value or yield forecast is produced.
