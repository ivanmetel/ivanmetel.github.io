01 / The task

Quality of service starts before work begins

A client asks for a fit-out proposal. The provider must shape a solution, even when detailed design documents are not yet available.

Delivering quality work is only part of the job. The provider also needs to respond quickly, understand the details and avoid passing unnecessary costs on to the client. That calls for one connected process for source data, estimating and presentation.

02 / Property data

From point cloud
to floor plan

The proposal depends on accurate source data. The provider needs a realistic contingency; the client needs a predictable price without an inflated buffer.

Interpreting the point cloud turns scan data into usable geometry: identify the walls, trace room boundaries, then calculate areas and perimeters. That accuracy feeds directly into the estimate.

In the source example, specialist scanning for an apartment of around 100 m² cost €1,500. For smaller projects, a compact LiDAR can provide enough data for an initial estimate without charging the client separately for a scan.
